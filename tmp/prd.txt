01  PROBLEM STATEMENT
Background
Gameday Men's Health is the nation's largest men's health clinic network with 400+ open locations. Each clinic is individually owned and is required to order prescriptions only from a small set of pre-approved network pharmacies. Today, each clinic maintains its own accounts and logins across roughly twelve different pharmacy portals. Staff manually log in to the correct portal to place each order, log back in to look up tracking numbers when patients call, and copy-paste those tracking numbers into text messages. Patients receive no automatic shipment notifications. The result is an antiquated, time-consuming, error-prone workflow on the clinic's single largest operating-expense category.
Problem
HeroScript replaces twelve pharmacy logins with one. For prescribers and their teams: a single portal, a unified product catalog, one order history, one customer-service relationship, and automated shipment notifications to patients. For pharmacies: one master account in place of 400 small ones, standardized ordering against a defined catalog, upstream compliance handling (DEA license tracking and expirations), simplified billing, and the consolidated volume of the entire Gameday network — which justifies preferred pricing. 
Why now
Clinics are actively asking for relief on their largest line-item expense. Pharmacies are asking for relief from hundreds of difficult low-volume accounts while wanting to retain the aggregate volume. Distribution is already solved: HeroScript launches with a built-in customer base inside the Gameday network and a co-founder/CEO who personally owns two of those clinics. Pain is acute, buyers are identified, suppliers are receptive, and ramp risk is low.
02  GOALS & NON-GOALS
Business goal
Reduce per-prescription cost for Gameday clinics by aggregating network demand into preferred pharmacy pricing; lift patient retention by enabling reliable refill workflows; and build a defensible, recurring-revenue marketplace with a sustainable network-level margin.
User goal
Replace fragmented multi-portal ordering with a single, faster, more reliable workflow that automatically informs patients when their prescriptions ship.
Non-goals
• HeroScript will not function as an EMR or store clinical chart data beyond what is needed to route an order.
• HeroScript will not recommend dosing, suggest medications, or make clinical decisions.
• HeroScript will not bill insurance or adjudicate Rx claims.
• HeroScript will not provide direct patient-facing clinical care or telehealth.
03  SCOPE
In scope (V1)
Onboarding & access
• HeroScript Ops provisions one Clinic Admin per clinic; the admin invites Prescribers and Medical Assistants and assigns roles and limits visibility and access by role/user (such as visibility to pricing).
• Roles: Clinic Admin, Prescriber, Medical Assistant, HeroScript Support.
• Multi-clinic prescribers supported (one prescriber can belong to multiple clinics; selects active clinic context when prescribing).
• Email + password authentication with MFA for all clinic users.
• ID.me identity verification step required at order submission for Prescribers for controlled substance prescriptions.
Patients & data
• Patient records entered manually once; persist for re-use and refill workflows.
• Patient shipping address verification via USPS / Smarty / Lob (inline suggestion UI).
Catalog, pharmacies & routing
• Unified product catalog managed by HeroScript Ops.
• Each product mapped to one or more eligible pharmacies, each with state-availability and a HeroScript-configured price (decoupled from underlying cost). Shipping is shown as a separate line item.
• State-licensing matrix per pharmacy; system filters available pharmacies to those licensed to ship to the patient's state.
• Clinician selects fulfilling pharmacy from the eligible list (unified catalog, manual pharmacy selection).
• API-only pharmacy integration. HeroScript Ops configures each pharmacy (LifeFile and proprietary variants). Up to 6 pharmacies live at V1 launch (per founder direction); remaining integrations roll in across V1.x toward the full ~12.
Order workflow
• MA composes a draft order → Prescriber reviews, completes ID.me step-up, and submits → order routes to the selected pharmacy via API.
• Order statuses tracked: Submitted → Accepted → In Process → Shipped (with tracking). "Delivered" is out of scope for V1; patients see Delivered status via the carrier tracking link.
• Multi-shipment support: a single order may produce multiple Shipments, each with its own tracking number. Patient notifications and order detail views handle "package N of M" framing.
Patient notifications
• SMS to patient on each shipment shipped, with carrier, tracking link, multi-shipment context, and clinic contact for questions. Email if it does not significantly add scope.
• Notifications never expose PHI beyond a tracking link. All patient communication directs questions back to the originating clinic (HeroScript and pharmacies do not field patient inquiries).
Refills (lite)
• System flags patients due for refill based on prior-order cadence. When order is placed, use rules to determine when refill will be needed (using dose vs vial size logic, BUD dates, and/or 28-day rule)
• One-click reorder pre-fills the prior order's product, quantity, prescriber, and shipping address. Prescriber still submits (ID.me) for compliance if controlled substance included (need to flag controlled meds in the catalog).
Compliance — V1
• HIPAA-eligible AWS hosting under a signed BAA. Encryption at rest and in transit.
• Role-based access control. Immutable audit log of all PHI access events and all order-state actions. 6-year retention.
• DEA compliance: prescriber uploads DEA license image and DEA number at onboarding; HeroScript Ops manually verifies against the DEA database during V1; expiration date stored; system automatically blocks expired prescribers; renewal reminders sent at 60 / 30 / 7 days before expiration, then ordering of controlled substances disabled until updated license uploaded and verified.
• BAAs executed with each integrated pharmacy and with the SMS / email provider (Twilio HIPAA tier or equivalent).
Clinic-facing reporting
• Order list with filters and search (status, date range, prescriber, patient, pharmacy).
• Refill / reminder queue with one-click reorder.
HeroScript Ops internal admin
• Onboard clinics and users (create, deactivate, role changes).
• Manage the unified product catalog and map products to fulfilling pharmacies. Each catalog item needs pre-created sigs that providers can use in the prescription. 
• Manage pharmacies, API credentials, catalog ingestion, and the state-licensing matrix.
• Monitor network-wide orders, intervene on stuck/failed orders (re-route, contact, cancel), all actions audit-logged.
Billing & payment collection
• HeroScript collects payment from clinics on a daily settlement cadence via credit card or ACH (no processor fee, preferred) or credit card (processor fee passed through to the clinic). PCI-compliant card handling, and a single integration covering both rails.
• Pharmacy payouts handled offline by HeroScript Finance (outside the platform in V1).
Platform
• Desktop web application built on existing stacks where possible (Tight UI frontend + Server backend) — pending agency selection.
Out of scope (V1)
• Mobile-responsive UI and native mobile apps (V1 is desktop web only).
• Patient portal / patient logins (V1 is notifications only).
• Full HeroScript-managed payment rails (collecting from clinics AND paying pharmacies inside the platform).
• EMR / practice-management integration (V1.5 target, planned shortly after V1).
• Automated DEA verification via Verisys or equivalent (V2 target, weeks after V1).
• Fully automated / scheduled recurring refills (V1 ships reminders + one-click reorder only).
• Pharmacy self-service portal (V1 is API-only + HeroScript Ops-managed onboarding).
• CSV exports and advanced analytics dashboards for clinics.
• Insurance billing / Rx claims adjudication.
• Telehealth or clinical consultation features.
• Gameday franchisor SSO / shared identity provider.
• Multi-language UI (English only in V1).
Assumptions
• V1 launches with ~10 pilot clinics from the Gameday network.
• V1 launches with up to 6 integrated pharmacies (per founder direction); additional pharmacies ramp in across V1.x toward a total of ~12.
• Pharmacy API documentation is available and has been collected for each launch pharmacy.
• HeroScript Ops is staffed to handle manual DEA verification, pharmacy onboarding, and order intervention during pilot.
• Pilot clinics commit to using HeroScript as their primary ordering tool.
• Patient SMS / email consent is captured implicitly via the clinic's intake forms; clinic affirms consent at order entry. Twilio standard "Reply STOP to opt out" wording included on every message. Final legal review of intake consent language recommended before broad rollout.
• Gameday franchisor provides tacit support of the pilot but is not in the build path; no integration or formal sign-off dependency.
Dependencies
• Pharmacy APIs: up to ~12 total integrations; up to 6 live at V1 launch. Mix of LifeFile-based and proprietary systems.
• Address verification: USPS / Smarty / Lob.
• SMS provider: Twilio HIPAA tier.
• Email provider: SendGrid HIPAA tier (or equivalent), if email is shipped in V1.
• Identity verification: ID.me (Prescriber step-up at submission; see Section 05 for cadence).
• Payment processing: Priority Payments (ACH + card, daily settlement).
• Hosting: HIPAA-eligible AWS account with executed BAA.
• Gameday franchisor's tacit support of the pilot (no integration dependency in V1).
04  SUCCESS METRICS
Primary metric
Total network pharmacy spend routed through HeroScript ($ per month).
Secondary metrics
• Average order volume per clinic (orders / clinic / month).
• Total clinics signed up and total clinics actively ordering (≥1 order / month).
• Percentage of clinics representing 50% of total order volume (concentration).
• Percentage of orders requiring human intervention by HeroScript Ops.
• Fulfillment speed — average and worst-case time from order submission to ship.
Guardrail metrics
• Help-desk tickets per 100 orders (initial target: < 5).
• Order rejection rate by pharmacy (target: < 2%).
• Address-verification failure / correction rate.
• SMS tracking-link delivery success rate (delivered / sent).
• DEA-expiration auto-block events (count, plus days early or late).
• Mean time to detect and resolve a stuck order (Submitted with no movement > X hours).
• Audit-log access events flagged for review (anomaly indicators).
Measurement plan
All events written to an append-only event log accessible to HeroScript Ops via downloadable raw CSV from the internal admin. A clinic-facing analytics dashboard is V2 scope; for V1, HeroScript Ops will produce weekly reports manually from the raw data.
05  USERS & ROLES
Roles and permissions
Clinic Admin
• Provisioned by HeroScript Ops at clinic onboarding (one per clinic).
• Invites users, assigns roles, manages clinic shipping addresses and clinic-level defaults.
• Views all orders for own clinic(s); cannot submit prescriptions unless also a Prescriber.
• Cannot access other clinics' data.
Prescriber
• Licensed clinician with DEA license on file.
• Creates and submits orders. Can also review and submit MA-drafted orders.
• Submission requires an ID.me identity-verification step.
• Multi-clinic membership: a Prescriber can belong to multiple clinics and selects active clinic context when prescribing.
• Automatic block on submission when DEA license is expired; renewal reminders at 60 / 30 / 7 days.
Medical Assistant (MA)
• Drafts orders on behalf of a Prescriber. Cannot submit.
• Limited PHI access — own clinic's patients only.
• Cannot manage users, catalog, or pharmacy configuration.
HeroScript Ops Admin
• HeroScript employees with access to internal admin: clinic & user onboarding, catalog, pharmacy integrations, and order monitoring + intervention.
• Highest-privilege role. All actions audit-logged.
• Sub-roles (Catalog, Pharmacy Ops, Support, Finance) defined post-V1 as the team grows.
Pharmacy (system integration)
• No UI in V1. Onboarded entirely by HeroScript Ops.
• Receives orders via API; returns acceptance, status updates, and shipment / tracking data via API or webhook.
Patient
• Not a user of the application in V1.
• Receives SMS (and optionally email) notifications with a tracking link on each shipment shipped.
• Questions are routed back to the originating clinic — HeroScript and pharmacies do not field patient inquiries.
Authentication & session policy
Email + password with MFA required for all clinic users; password reset via verified email.
Prescriber submission requires an ID.me identity verification step. Default cadence is per-submission (industry / EPCS norm for controlled substances). HeroScript intends to reduce this to once-per-session if legal counsel confirms it is acceptable for the V1 controlled-substance catalog — flagged as a Section 13 open item.
HeroScript Ops Admin sessions enforce stronger MFA and short idle timeout. IP-allowlist optional but recommended.
All sessions tracked in the audit log (login, logout, idle expiry, role escalation).
06  KEY USER JOURNEYS
Clinic onboarding
HeroScript Ops creates the clinic record, captures clinic address(es), and provisions one Clinic Admin account.
Clinic Admin receives activation email, sets password + MFA, signs into the portal.
Clinic Admin invites Prescribers (Prescribers complete DEA upload during signup) and MAs.
DEA collection & verification (V1 manual)
Prescriber uploads DEA license image + DEA number during account activation.
Account remains in pending-submission state until HeroScript Ops verifies the DEA against the DEA database and records the expiration.
Once verified, Prescriber can submit orders.
Order entry (MA)
MA selects or creates a patient record.
MA selects active clinic shipping address (or enters / verifies a custom patient shipping address via Smarty / Lob suggestions).
System filters available pharmacies to those licensed to ship to the patient's state.
MA selects product(s) from the unified catalog; system shows eligible pharmacies side-by-side with HeroScript price and shipping fee.
MA picks fulfilling pharmacy and saves the draft for the Prescriber.
Order submission (Prescriber)
Prescriber sees pending drafts on the dashboard.
Prescriber reviews patient + items, completes the ID.me step-up, and submits.
Order is sent to the selected pharmacy via API. Audit log records actor, timestamp, payload hash.
Pharmacy fulfillment & status updates
Pharmacy returns Accepted (or Rejected with reason) via API.
Pharmacy returns In Process and then Shipped events with per-shipment tracking numbers.
For multi-item orders that ship as multiple packages, each Shipment is tracked independently.
HeroScript updates the order detail view in real time (or near-real time, depending on webhook vs. polling).
Patient notification
On each Shipment Shipped event, HeroScript sends an SMS (and optionally email) to the patient with carrier, tracking link, "package N of M" context if applicable, and the originating clinic's contact info.
No PHI in the message beyond the tracking link.
Refill reminder & one-click reorder
System flags patients approaching their refill window based on prior-order cadence (default 30 days; adjustable in V1.x).
Clinic sees the patient in the refill queue.
MA or Prescriber clicks Reorder; system pre-fills product, quantity, prescriber, and shipping address from the prior order.
Prescriber submits with ID.me as in a standard order.
DEA expiration handling
System checks DEA and state medical license expiration daily.
60 / 30 / 7-day reminders sent to Prescriber and Clinic Admin.
On the expiration date, the Prescriber is blocked from order submission until a new license is uploaded and verified.
HeroScript Ops intervention on a stuck order
Ops monitor flags orders Submitted > X hours without acceptance, or any order returning Rejected.
Ops opens the order, reviews API exchange logs, contacts pharmacy if needed, and either re-routes to an alternate eligible pharmacy or cancels with notes back to the clinic.
All Ops actions audit-logged.
07  FEATURE SPECIFICATIONS
F1  Unified product catalog
HeroScript Ops maintains a normalized product catalog (name, strength, form, SKU, image, description, controlled-substance flag).
Each product has 1..N PharmacyMappings; each mapping carries: pharmacy ID, pharmacy SKU, HeroScript price (configurable, independent of cost), shipping fee, lead-time estimate, and an active flag.
Catalog search and filter by name, category, controlled flag.
F2  State-licensing matrix
Each pharmacy carries a list of states it is licensed to ship to (with optional effective and expiration dates).
Order entry filters available pharmacies by the patient's shipping-address state.
Internal admin surface to view, edit, and audit changes to the matrix.
F3  Order entry workflow
Draft state owned by MA (or Prescriber). Multiple items per order.
Eligible-pharmacy panel shows side-by-side price, shipping, and lead-time for the selected items.
Validation: prescriber DEA active, patient address verified, pharmacy licensed in patient's state, all items available.
F4  Prescriber submission + ID.me step-up
Submission triggers an ID.me verification step. Default cadence is per-submission for controlled substances (industry / EPCS norm); HeroScript may reduce this to once-per-session if legal counsel confirms acceptability.
On success, order moves to Submitted state and the pharmacy API is called.
On failure or cancellation, order remains in pending submission.
The system stores the ID.me verification ID with each submission for audit purposes.
F5  Pharmacy integration adapter layer
Each pharmacy implementation lives behind a common internal interface (placeOrder, fetchStatus, cancelOrder, fetchCatalog).
Per-pharmacy adapters handle LifeFile and proprietary variants. Credentials and endpoints managed by HeroScript Ops.
Retries with backoff, dead-letter queue for failed submissions, idempotency keys to prevent duplicates.
F6  Order status & multi-shipment tracking
Order has Status (Submitted / Accepted / In Process / Shipped / Rejected / Cancelled). "Delivered" is NOT tracked inside HeroScript in V1 — patients see Delivered status via the carrier tracking link.
Order has 0..N Shipments. Each Shipment carries carrier, tracking number, status, ship date, contained items.
Order Status derived from Shipment states (e.g., Order is Shipped when at least one Shipment ships; All-Shipped flag once final shipment ships).
F7  Patient notifications
On each Shipment Shipped event, send SMS (Twilio HIPAA tier) with tracking link, package N of M, and clinic contact info.
Optional email on Shipped if it does not materially expand scope; same minimal payload.
No PHI in message body. Link target is a carrier tracking page (not a HeroScript-hosted page in V1).
F8  Address verification
Inline suggestion UI during patient shipping-address entry using USPS / Smarty / Lob.
Verified addresses stored on the patient record with last-verified timestamp.
F9  DEA license management
Prescriber profile carries DEA number, license image, status, expiration date, last-verified-by, last-verified-on.
Daily expiration check; auto-block on expiry; reminder cadence 60 / 30 / 7 days.
Manual verification UI in HeroScript Ops admin (V1). Verisys / equivalent integration is V2.
F10  Refill reminder + one-click reorder
Refill cadence default 28 days; configurable per product / per patient in V1.x.
Calculate refill dates based on dosing (amount and frequency) vs vial size, BUD for the product, and/or 28-day rule (injectable medications expire 28 days after puncture).
Clinic-facing refill queue lists patients approaching refill window.
One-click reorder duplicates prior order (items, prescriber, shipping address); Prescriber submits with ID.me.
F11  Clinic-facing order list & search
Filterable by status, date range, prescriber, patient, pharmacy.
Order detail view shows full timeline, shipment(s), tracking, API exchange summary (Ops-facing detail hidden from clinic).
F12  HeroScript Ops internal admin
Clinic onboarding + user management.
Product catalog management + pharmacy mapping.
Pharmacy onboarding: credentials, endpoint config, catalog ingestion, state-licensing matrix.
Order monitor: network-wide view, stuck-order alerts, intervention actions (re-route, contact, cancel).
DEA verification queue.
Raw-event / audit-log download.
F13  Billing & clinic payment collection (Stripe)
HeroScript collects payment from clinics on a daily settlement cadence via ACH (no processor fee, preferred) or credit card (processor fee passed through to the clinic).
Clinic admin sees daily settlement history in the portal: orders included, amount charged, payment method, status.
Receipts / consolidated monthly statements emailed to the clinic billing contact.
Pharmacy payouts handled offline in V1 by HeroScript Finance (out of platform).
Priority Payments chosen as the V1 payment processor: ACH + card on a single integration, daily B2B-ready settlement, PCI-compliant card handling.
F14  Audit logging
All PHI access events and all order-state actions written to an immutable append-only log.
Captured fields: actor, role, clinic context, action, target entity ID, timestamp, source IP, request ID, hash of payload.
Retention: 6 years.

Internal control room for HeroScript staff. Designed for fast triage and intervention.

Screen 6  —  Prescriber Profile (DEA management)
Prescriber's own profile view; also accessible to HeroScript Ops for verification.

Screen 7  —  Patient SMS Template (notification copy)
The only patient-facing surface in V1. No PHI in the body beyond a tracking link.
Sample copy (single-shipment): "Hi Sarah — your prescription from Gameday Logan has shipped via FedEx. Track it here: <link>. Questions? Call your clinic at (435) 555-0199."
Sample copy (multi-shipment, first of two): "Hi Sarah — package 1 of 2 from your Gameday Logan order has shipped via FedEx. Track it: <link>. The second package will ship separately and you'll get a separate text."
Sample copy (multi-shipment, second of two): "Hi Sarah — package 2 of 2 from your Gameday Logan order has shipped via UPS. Track it: <link>. Both packages are now on the way."
Strict template: no medication names, no condition references, no dosing details. Pharmacy name not exposed to patient by default (questions go to clinic).

11  NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
Compliance (HIPAA)
• HIPAA-eligible AWS services only; BAA in place before any PHI lands.
• Encryption at rest and in transit; key management via AWS KMS.
• Role-based access control; principle of least privilege.
• Immutable append-only audit log with 6-year retention.
• BAAs with every downstream PHI-touching vendor (pharmacies, Twilio, SendGrid, ID.me, Smarty/Lob).
• No PHI in URLs, logs, error messages, or non-HIPAA telemetry.
• No PHI sent to AI/LLM systems.
Security
• MFA for all clinic and Ops users; stronger MFA for Ops.
• Session idle timeout; short for Ops.
• Secrets stored in AWS Secrets Manager; no secrets in code or repos.
• Per-environment isolation; production access gated and audited.
• Annual penetration test; quarterly internal review (post-pilot).
Performance
• Page-to-interactive < 2 seconds for primary clinic flows under normal load.
• Order submission round-trip (HeroScript → pharmacy adapter → ack) < 5 seconds typical; queued with retries if pharmacy is slow.
• Notification dispatch within 1 minute of receiving Shipped event from pharmacy.
Reliability
• Target uptime 99.5% during V1 pilot; raise to 99.9% as the platform scales beyond the pilot cohort.
• Daily encrypted backups; documented restore runbook.
• Dead-letter queue and Ops alerting for failed pharmacy API calls.
Scalability
• Architected for 400 clinics, 12 pharmacies, and projected V1 → V2 order volume without re-platforming.
• Stateless app tier behind a load balancer; database with read replicas as load grows.
Accessibility & browser support
• WCAG 2.1 AA target.
• Latest two major versions of Chrome, Edge, Safari, and Firefox.
• Desktop web only in V1; mobile-responsive deferred.
Data retention
• PHI and audit logs retained 6 years (HIPAA).
• Patient deletion / data-portability requests handled out-of-band by HeroScript Ops in V1; user-facing flows are V2.
Localization

Glossary
BAA
Business Associate Agreement — HIPAA-required agreement with any vendor handling PHI on HeroScript's behalf.
DEA
Drug Enforcement Administration — license required to prescribe controlled substances; tracked per Prescriber with expiration.
EMR
Electronic Medical Records system — out of scope for V1; integration targeted for V1.5.
LifeFile
Common pharmacy-management software used by several network pharmacies; API capabilities vary by deployment.
MA
Medical Assistant — drafts orders for a Prescriber but cannot submit.
PHI
Protected Health Information — any patient-identifying health data; covered by HIPAA.
Prescriber
Licensed clinician authorized to submit orders; requires DEA on file and ID.me verification at submission.
