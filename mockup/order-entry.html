New Order

Order = basket. An order fulfills one or more existing or new Prescriptions at one pharmacy, shipping to one address.

Top-down flow: patient & ship-to → prescription lines (existing Rxs or new Rxs) → eligible pharmacy → review & submit. The pharmacy panel only unlocks once at least one line is added — eligibility is computed from the basket.
Alternative workflow available — Separated Procurement → In the alternative model, prescribers approve individual orders and a procurement operator picks pharmacy(ies) in batch from the Procurement Queue. See comparison.
