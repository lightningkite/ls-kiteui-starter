Procurement Queue

Approved prescriptions awaiting pharmacy selection and dispatch. Procurement operator groups them by destination, picks pharmacy(ies), and confirms — that's what creates the PharmacyOrder and triggers dispatch.

Role split in this workflow: Prescribers approve clinical content; ClinicAdmin (or a dedicated procurement role) picks pharmacies. Enables clinical-shipping batching — one bulk shipment to the clinic containing many patients' Rxs, packing-optimized. Trade-off: an extra human step between approval and dispatch.

Why Separated Procurement over Combined Basket? The procurement role split + destination grouping pays off when clinic-bulk-shipping is common (5+ Rxs/day per clinic landing at the clinic address, not patient addresses). When most shipments go direct-to-patient anyway, the extra screen is overhead. See Model comparison for the trade-off matrix.
